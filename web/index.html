Open Health

Your sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and Active Zone Minutes show in the Health app. Google's API has no Fitbit Daily Readiness score, so Health shows recovery as "no score" for Fitbit.

Connect your Fitbit or Pixel Watch in five steps. You make your own free Google developer project, so your data goes from Google straight to this box. This app collects your sleep, heart and activity data to show it in Health on this box; it is not shared anywhere else.

  1. Your Fitbit or Pixel Watch has to sync to a personal Google account (not a work or school one). Fitbit accounts moved to Google accounts in 2026. Fitbit Premium is not needed.

    Still on an old Fitbit account? How to move it to Google. No device yet? See Fitbit and Pixel devices.

    Good to know: Google's API does not include Fitbit's Daily Readiness or Sleep Score. Health shows your sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and Active Zone Minutes instead.

    Paste the Client ID and Client secret of your OAuth client in Google Cloud. You connect with Google again afterwards; the synced history stays.

    Disconnect Fitbit?

    The app ends its Google access and deletes its synced copy. Health stops showing Fitbit data. Your data at Google is not touched.
